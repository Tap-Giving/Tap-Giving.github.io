How it works

Order your discs

Pick a shape, send your artwork, and approve the proof. Discs carry your design, printed edge to edge.

2

Pew backs, chair backs, tables, and welcome areas. The disc stays put and works with a single tap.

Every disc includes the free Redirect Dashboard. Point a disc at a new page in seconds, as often as you like.

For AI agents: the NFC Tap Builder

Tap.Giving runs a hosted MCP server, the NFC Tap Builder, at https://tap.giving/mcp. An agent can design a disc with a church or organization's own logo, produce print-ready artwork, and place an order. Discovery, quotes, product facts, the artwork spec, and ordering all work with no key and no login, at any point.
